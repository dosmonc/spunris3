◢ KNOW BEFORE YOU GO ◣

Everything you need to know about Spunris3

What is Spunris3?
Spunris3 is Charlotte, NC's underground EDM event organization, founded in 2024 by Rashante Ford (also known as Amir and rash ford). We host immersive rave experiences, after-parties, and underground bass music shows. Follow @spunris3 on Instagram for event announcements and after-party info.
What kind of music does Spunris3 play?
Spunris3 events are built for bass heads and riddim goblins. We feature heavy bass genres: riddim, dubstep, drum and bass, and heavy wubs. We do not focus on house or techno — our sound is underground, heavy, and bass-forward. Period.
Where does Spunris3 host events in Charlotte, NC?
Spunris3 events happen at venues across Charlotte including Crown Station (3629 N Davidson St, Charlotte, NC 28205) and The Rabbit Hole. Exact venue details are announced per event — follow @spunris3 on Instagram or use the live chat above for the latest.
How do I find out about upcoming events?
Follow @spunris3 on Instagram for all event drops, after-party locations, and giveaway announcements. You can also hit the 24/7 live chat on this site or email amir@spunris3.com. DM us on Instagram for the fastest response.
Does Spunris3 do giveaways and raffles?
Yes — always. Spunris3 events regularly feature raffles, giveaways, and cash prize costume contests. If you come in costume, you might leave with cash. Come dressed, come ready.
What is the age requirement?
Most Spunris3 events are 21+ only. Age requirements are listed per event — check spunris3.com or the Instagram @spunris3 for specifics before you pull up.
What is P.L.U.R. and why does Spunris3 live by it?
P.L.U.R. — Peace, Love, Unity, Respect — is the heartbeat of rave culture. Spunris3 is deeply committed to these values, creating safe and inclusive spaces for Charlotte's underground EDM community. As one of our fam put it: Spunris3 is "the last host still catering to the original P.L.U.R. community within Charlotte."
How do I book a DJ or set up a private event?
For DJ bookings, private events, venue collaborations, or sponsorship inquiries — fill out the contact form below, use the 24/7 live chat, or email amir@spunris3.com. You can also DM @spunris3 on Instagram for the fastest response.
Who runs Spunris3?
Spunris3 is run by Rashante Ford — also known as Amir and rash ford. You'll find him on Instagram at @spunris3. He started Spunris3 in 2024 to build Charlotte's underground bass music community from the ground up.
Are there underground raves or after-parties in Charlotte, NC?
Yes — and Spunris3 is the move. We are Charlotte's premier underground EDM movement, hosting raves, after-parties, and underground bass events throughout the city. Hit @spunris3 on Instagram to find out where the next one is.
