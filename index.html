Spunris3 Beats Q&A

  • What kind of raves does Spunris3 host?

    Spunris3 hosts immersive underground EDM events featuring heavy bass music — riddim, dubstep, drum and bass, and heavy wubs. Our events cater to bass heads and riddim goblins with a strong focus on P.L.U.R. (Peace, Love, Unity, Respect) rave culture. Each event features live DJs, raffles, costume contests, games, and vibrant energy.

  • How do I find upcoming Spunris3 events?

    Follow @spunris3 on Instagram for all event announcements, after-party details, and giveaways. You can also visit spunris3.com or use our 24/7 live chat for event info. For bookings or direct inquiries, email amir@spunris3.com.

  • What is the age requirement?

    Most Spunris3 events are 21+ only to ensure a mature, safe environment. Check spunris3.com or Instagram @spunris3 for event-specific age requirements, as some special events may vary.

  • Do you offer raffles and giveaways?

    Yes! Spunris3 events feature regular raffles, giveaways, and cash prize costume contests. Rewarding the most creative ravers is a core part of the Spunris3 experience. Follow @spunris3 on Instagram for giveaway announcements!

  • Can I book Spunris3 for my event?

    Absolutely! For DJ bookings, private events, venue collaborations, or partnerships, reach out via our contact form on spunris3.com, our 24/7 live chat, or email amir@spunris3.com. We'd love to bring the bass to your event!
