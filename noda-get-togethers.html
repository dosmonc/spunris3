Kandi Trading Culture In NoDa

Kandi trading is alive and well in Charlotte's underground scene, and Spunris3 get togethers are where it happens organically. Show up with your handmade kandi bracelets and singles — you'll leave with new ones on your wrists and new friends in your contacts.

The PLUR handshake, the trade, the exchange — it's one of the most genuine rituals in underground music culture and it's part of every Spunris3 gathering. If you're new to kandi culture, the community will teach you. Everyone was new once.
